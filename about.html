About Majestic Excalibur

A global energy and commodity trading company built on integrity, transparency and long-term partnership.

Our Company

Connecting reliable supply with global demand

Majestic Excalibur Oil & Gas Trading LLC operates across global energy and commodity markets, supplying petroleum products, gas, lubricants, bitumen, coal, crude oil and agricultural commodities through reliable sourcing and compliant trading practices.

Our capabilities are supported by decades of experience, international partnerships and a diversified product portfolio. We source from internationally certified producers and refineries across multiple regions, serving industrial, commercial and government clients with professionally managed trade execution.

Built on ethical conduct and transparency, we combine market insight with operational discipline to deliver consistent value in complex international trading environments.

What Drives Us

Mission, vision & values

Our Mission

Our Vision

Our Values

Integrity, transparency, efficiency and accountability guide every transaction. We trade responsibly and hold ourselves to the highest professional standards.
