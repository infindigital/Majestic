Who We Are

A Global Energy and Commodities Trading Company

Majestic Excalibur Oil & Gas Trading LLC is a global energy and commodity trading company focused on sourcing, supplying, and distributing high quality products across international markets. We work with trusted producers, refineries, and partners to deliver petroleum products, gas, lubricants, crude oil, coal, bitumen, and agricultural commodities with consistency and compliance.

With a disciplined trading approach and strong market understanding, we support industrial, commercial, and government clients through reliable supply chains and transparent business practices. Our operations are guided by integrity, efficiency, and long term partnership values.
