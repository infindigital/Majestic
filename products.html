Energy & Commodities

Sourced responsibly. Supplied reliably.

Every product is backed by certified specifications, independent inspection and professionally managed logistics — from refinery to destination.

Crude Oil

Premium-grade crude sourced from reliable producers to support refinery operations and international energy markets. Available in a range of grades and volumes.

EN590 / ULSD Diesel

Ultra-low-sulfur diesel in 10 ppm specification, suitable for automotive, industrial and marine applications while meeting international fuel standards.

Gasoil / Middle Distillate

A refined middle distillate fuel widely used across industrial and power-generation sectors, supplied with consistent quality and regulatory compliance.

Jet Fuel (Jet A-1)

Aviation turbine fuel supplied to meet international aviation standards for safe, efficient and reliable flight operations.

LNG & Natural Gas

Clean-burning natural gas supplied in liquefied form for efficient storage, transportation and industrial energy use.

Heavy Fuel Oil (HFO)

Heavy fuel oils suitable for marine engines, power plants and industrial boilers, supplied to required viscosity and sulfur grades.

Bitumen

High-quality bitumen products supplied for road construction, infrastructure development and industrial use, in penetration and viscosity grades.

Naphtha

Light petroleum distillate used as a feedstock in petrochemical processing, blending and industrial applications.

Lubricants & Marine Fuels

Comprehensive marine fuel and lubricant solutions supporting shipping and offshore operations with dependable quality.

Coal

Thermal and energy-grade coal supplied to power generation and industrial buyers, sourced from established producers.

Agricultural Commodities

Sourcing and trade of selected agricultural commodities, supporting diversified international supply requirements.

Petrochemical Feedstocks

Base petrochemical products and blending components for downstream processing and industrial manufacturing.
